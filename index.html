A little closer, even from far away

Because home still matters, wherever life takes you.

For Kenyans living abroad, caring for family and keeping up with responsibilities back home can mean many calls, messages and moving pieces. For busy households in Kenya, finding time to coordinate everyday tasks can be just as challenging.

Glenora Homes is here to make those arrangements easier. We listen to what matters, help coordinate the practical details and keep communication clear along the way.

Every request is discussed and confirmed individually. Services, availability, costs and timelines are agreed with the client before work starts.
